📊 Product Information

GanttManagementSheet - Project and Task Planning

Transform your Google Sheets™ into a dynamic project management tool with this powerful Gantt Chart add-on. Perfect for project managers, teams, and freelancers who need professional project visualization without complex software.

✓ Free with Premium Features

Key Features

  • Interactive Gantt Charts – Automatically plot tasks with start/end dates, durations, progress tracking, assignees, and dependencies
  • One-Click Generation – Generate professional Gantt charts in seconds with a single click
  • Customizable Settings – Map spreadsheet columns to task details for seamless integration with your existing workflows
  • Task Dependencies & Milestones – Visualize critical paths and project bottlenecks
  • Progress Tracking – Monitor percentage completion for each task
  • Responsive Design – Easy viewing on any device
  • No Coding Required – Works directly in Google Sheets™ with an intuitive interface
Perfect For:

Project planning • Agile workflows • Construction timelines • Academic research • Event management • Workflow tracking • Team coordination

How It Works

  1. Install the add-on from Google Workspace Marketplace
  2. Configure your task data in Google Sheets
  3. Map your columns to task properties
  4. Click to generate your interactive Gantt chart
  5. Share and collaborate with your team!
🔒 Privacy & Permissions: This add-on requires access to view and manage spreadsheets where it's installed, send emails on your behalf (for notifications), and connect to external services for enhanced functionality. Your data remains secure and is never shared with third parties.
